How it all started

We've been doing this work since 2014. We've managed projects in the United States and across three East African countries. Along the way, we've relied on the same kinds of tools we now help our clients use — cloud services like Microsoft 365™, OneDrive™, and Google Cloud™, and, more recently, practical AI tools like Microsoft Copilot™ and Claude™ — to work faster and smarter.

Technology has always been part of how we work. One of us has been building websites for over 26 years, and we both have stayed hands-on with technology the whole way through — learning, adapting, and keeping up as tools have changed.

This business grew out of all of that — real project management experience and a genuine, long-standing comfort with technology. We started this business to bring that same practical, organized, tech-supported approach to small business owners dealing with the same kinds of challenges we've spent years solving — too many moving parts, not enough time, and a feeling that there has to be an easier way.

We're not consultants who learned this from a textbook. We've done the work, in different countries and different industries, using the same kinds of tools we now help our clients use — and we bring all of that real-world experience to every client we work with.
